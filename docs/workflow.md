# Swap Work Flow

This document provides a high-level overview of of the operation of the Ski Swap. The workflow consists of 3 general areas:

1. Vendor and inventory checkin
1. Sales
1. Unsold equipment pickup / Vendor closeout

Additional steps performed by Swap Administator(s):

1. Pre-swap data initialization
1. Website inventory update
1. Post swap
    1. Data close-out
    1. Check printing
    1. Final reports

## Vendor and Inventory Check In

People (who we refer to as Vendors) will bring in equipment they wish to sell. They will fill out a consignment contract which includes name, address, etc., and a list their items with description and price. We post the form on our website, so some vendors come in with the form already filled out. Our staff help them label and price their items, and affix barcode labels to each item. Vendors have the option to donate any item that does not sell. 

When the form is completed, and the items labeled, the vendor proceeds to one of the check-in workstations, where staff will key in their vendor information, then enter each of the itmes to be sold. A vendor check-in sheet is printed and given to the vendor, and we keep the original form for our records. 

A small number of vendors come with spreadsheet containing all of their items on a flash drive. We can import this spreadsheet into our database to save typing. 

We charge a commission of 23% for all vendors, except patrollers who are charged a discounted rate of 15%.

## Sales

Sales are handled at any workstation. We accept cash, check, credit card, and Venmo (maybe?). A 3% surcharge is added to orders paying by credit card or Venmo. We also offer a 10% discount for patrollers.

Sales receipts are printed on our receipt printers.

## Vendor pickup and closeout

At the end of the swap, vendors return to pick up any items that have not sold AND have been marked for donation. When all items are either sold, returned to the vendor or donated, the vendor is considered closed out.

## Admin functions

### Pre-sale
The admin staff will perform initial setup of the database. This function will clear out prior swap inventory and sales data. Vendor data will be retained, and all vendors marked as inactive. This allows us to re-activate returning vendors with re-entering their data. 

### Website update
We keep a list of inventory status on our website so that vendors can check to see if their items have sold. Updating this data from the active swap database is currently a manual process, and is performed periodically through the durration of the swap.

### Post-sale

#### Check Printing
Checks are printed after the closing of the swap, and mailed out to each vendor who has sold items. Thus, it is important that accurate mailing address is collected and entered for each vendor.

#### Close out
After the sale is completed, all inventory that is not sold AND is flaged as Donate if Not Sold is moved to Donated status. 

#### Final Reports
Reports are available in the system in the form of spreadsheets. They are:
1. Donated items. This assists staff in pulling donated items aside.
1. Sales tax. Total sales tax collected for reporting to the state.
1. Sales by register.
1. Employee discounts.
1. Payout Report. Lists every check to be written - items consigned, items sold, commission withheld, and net payout.
